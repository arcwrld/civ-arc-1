What Hewn checks — 13 rules, and what it does not check

    Hewn reads the shape of your data, not its meaning. It cannot tell you which date convention is right, only that two of them are mixed. It does not check values against a schema, and it does not read Excel files.

    Hewn 1.0.0 · one file, no server, no install, no network, no dependencies. The same rules run from a terminal: node tools/hewn/hewn.js <file>. Part of the Kit.
